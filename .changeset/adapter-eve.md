`@getdonn/adapter-sdk` gains the `AgentAdapter` contract, its conformance kit and `PortabilityService`. `@getdonn/adapter-eve` is new: it compiles a persona into the files of an eve agent (instructions, skills, MCP connections and model routing through a gateway), with an ownership manifest and a portability report per template.

`docs/capabilities.md` and `donn capabilities --markdown` list the agent adapters beside the harnesses.
